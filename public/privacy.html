Privacy Policy

Bill C-27 Compliant

Effective date: March 2026 · Camino North Projects

1. Who We Are

ClearCart is operated by Camino North Projects, a British Columbia company. Our designated Privacy Officer can be reached at privacy@caminonorth.ca.

2. What We Collect

  • Account data: Email address, full name, and Vancouver postal code provided at sign-up.
  • Receipt images: Photos of grocery receipts uploaded by you for price extraction. Images are stored in Supabase cloud storage.
  • Price data: Grocery item names and prices extracted from your receipts.
  • Shopping preferences: Your Triple Constraint focus (Cost / Time / Health), storage capacity setting, and strict health mode toggle.
  • Recipes: Recipe data imported by you from third-party URLs.
  • Community flags: Items you choose to flag as questionable pricing, shared with the Vancouver community feed.

3. Why We Collect It

  • To compare grocery prices and help you find the lowest-cost, closest, or healthiest store option.
  • To award ClearCredits for scanning receipts at local independent stores.
  • To surface community-flagged pricing data to help Vancouver shoppers save money.
  • To provide recipe management and smart ingredient import features.

4. How Long We Keep It

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days. Community-flagged price data may be retained in anonymized, aggregated form for up to 12 months for public interest reporting.

5. Who Sees Your Data

Your personal data is only accessible to:

  • You, through the ClearCart app.
  • Camino North Projects staff with a legitimate operational need (e.g., technical support).
  • Supabase Inc. (our cloud infrastructure provider), under their Data Processing Agreement.

We never sell, share, or monetize your personal data with third parties.

6. Your Rights Under Bill C-27

Under Canada's Consumer Privacy Protection Act (Bill C-27), you have the right to:

  • Access: Download a complete export of your personal data from Profile → Download My Data.
  • Correction: Update your name and postal code directly in the Profile settings.
  • Erasure: Permanently delete your account and all associated data from Profile → Delete My Account & Data.
  • Complaint: Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

7. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Database access is protected by Row Level Security policies — your data is never accessible by other users. Receipt images are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket with access-controlled download URLs.

8. Cookies

ClearCart uses only a single session cookie set by Supabase Auth to keep you logged in. No advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies are used.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you via in-app notice before the changes take effect. Continued use of ClearCart after that date constitutes acceptance.
